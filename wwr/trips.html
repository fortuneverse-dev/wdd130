Equipment We Provide

Your safety and comfort are important to us. RiverNest Rafting provides essential equipment for every adventure.

Safety Helmets

Certified helmets help protect your head while navigating the river.

Life Jackets

Properly fitted personal flotation devices are provided for every participant.

Paddles

Each participant receives a paddle designed for comfortable and effective river navigation.

Rafting Boats

Our durable inflatable rafts are designed for different river conditions and group sizes.

What Should You Bring?

To make your rafting experience comfortable, we recommend bringing the following items:

  • Comfortable clothing
  • Water shoes or secure sandals
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Sunscreen
  • Hat or sunglasses with a secure strap
  • Dry clothes for after the trip
